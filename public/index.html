    How to play Fish

    • Teams. Six players, two teams of three. Teammates sit alternately, and all 54 cards (including the 8s and both Jokers) are dealt out, 9 each.
    • Half-suits. The deck is split into nine sets of six: Low (2–7) and High (9–A) of each suit, plus the 8s and Jokers.
    • Asking. On your turn, ask an opponent for a specific card. You must already hold at least one card from that half-suit, and not the card you're asking for. If they have it, they hand it over and you go again. If not, the turn passes to them.
    • Declaring. When your team thinks it holds a whole half-suit, declare it by naming which teammate holds each card. Right: your team scores. Wrong: the other team does.
    • Out of cards? If it's your turn and your hand is empty, pass the turn to a teammate who still has cards.
    • Winning. The game ends once all nine half-suits are declared. Most half-suits wins.
